> INFO _

A pocket tag machine for graffiti writers, built after the original "Tags" app and the KATSU app.

Both are old and quiet now. The canvas saves as a PNG with a transparent background, and you can choose from 30 backgrounds and a full colour range.

No ads, no subscription, no account, nothing to sign up for. Your drawing stays on this device, and nothing loads over the network while you draw. Built with standard Android tools.
